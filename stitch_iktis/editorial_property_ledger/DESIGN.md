## Brand & Style

This design system crafts an atmosphere of calm authority and architectural permanence for private landlords and discerning property owners. Rather than relying on generic software tropes, vivid synthetic gradients, or cluttered dashboard panels, the interface is modeled after a curated architectural monograph: measured, quiet, and tactile.

The emotional signature is unhurried composure. Financial ledgers, lease milestones, and rent receipts are treated not as noisy transactional notifications, but as considered records of value. Every layout establishes hierarchy through confident proportion, deliberate whitespace, and quiet structural lines rather than decorative chrome.

### Design Movement: Warm Minimalist Modernism
- **Architectural Framing:** Subtle structural borders frame spaces like fine joinery. Clean dividers establish boundaries without visual weight.
- **Editorial Contrast:** Contemporary rounded display figures command attention for capital totals and property addresses, paired with crisp, clean sans-serif metrics for operational utility.
- **Calm Warmth:** A warm stone and limestone canvas replaces sterile cold whites, lending an organic, grounded physical quality to digital management.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in noble architectural materials: limestone, hand-pressed paper, patinated slate, warm oak embers, and weathered terracotta.

### Core Roles
- **Primary Canvas (`#EEE9DF` / Palladian):** The master ground plane. Soft, matte, and light-absorbing, shielding the user from eye fatigue and providing a tactile, non-digital foundation.
- **Structural Surfaces & Lines (`#C9C1B1` / Oatmeal):** Applied to card fills, structural hairline borders, muted metadata chips, and hairline table dividers. Used at variable opacities (notably `rgba(201, 193, 177, 0.4)`) for subtle grid articulation.
- **Brand Navy (`#2C3B4D` / Blue Fantastic):** The primary brand voice for navigation titles, secondary headers, active state iconography, and contextual badges.
- **Deep Anchor (`#1B2632` / Abyssal Anchofish Blue):** The primary color anchor. Delivers uncompromising contrast for key currency totals, primary headlines, active navigation states, and deep interactive surfaces.
- **Focal Accent (`#FFB162` / Burning Flame):** A refined, warm amber flame used sparingly for primary interactive buttons, critical action triggers, and positive yield indicators.
- **Restrained Caution (`#A35139` / Truffle Trouble):** A muted, terracotta earth tone designated strictly for past-due balances, lease expirations, maintenance disruptions, and critical alerts. Never neon red; always earthen and dignified.

## Typography

Typography establishes the balance between high-end architectural gravitas and systematic operational utility. 

- **Display & Financial Values (`Comfortaa`):** Used for primary portfolio yields, monthly aggregate rental revenues, and building estate addresses. Its rounded, modern forms provide a softer, approachable authority and deliberate visual weight.
- **Interface & Operational Text (`DM Sans`):** Used for body copy, tabular lists, and operational descriptions. DM Sans provides exceptional legibility and a contemporary rhythm.
- **Labels & Micro-Copy (`Outfit`):** Used for navigation chrome, technical tenant status tokens, and metadata fields. Rendered in uppercase with generous tracking (`0.05em` to `0.08em`) to delineate metadata fields (e.g., `UNIT 4B`, `LEASE TERMINATION`, `ESCROW DEPOSIT`) with architectural precision.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is conceived mobile-first, prioritizing one-handed thumb range, rapid ledger scanning, and concise inspection workflows.

### Grid & Structure
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column flow with `1rem` outer canvas margin. Component spacing relies on strict vertical rhythm (`space-md` between cards, `space-sm` inside grouping clusters).
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 6-column grid with `1.5rem` gutters and `2rem` outer margins. Property listings adopt side-by-side metric layouts.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** Fixed-width centered viewport (maximum 1280px) over a 12-column architectural grid with `1.5rem` gutters. Side panels house contextual tenant dossiers and historical transaction ledgers without obscuring parent context.

### Bottom Navigation
The mobile shell anchors the four core tabs—**Dashboard**, **Properties**, **Tenants**, **Payments**—in a floating navigation bar resting above the home indicator, separated by a hairline border without heavy blur or drop shadows.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects blurry drop shadows, tinted gradients, and synthetic glassmorphism. Instead, depth is conveyed through tactile structural boundaries and light tonal layering.

- **The Plane Model:** The primary canvas (`#EEE9DF`) sits at ground level. Elevated cards and drawers utilize `#F4EFE6` or `#FFFFFF` to distinguish active planes, framed cleanly by hairline outlines (`1px solid rgba(201, 193, 177, 0.4)`).
- **Outlines Over Shadows:** Components rely on crisp borders rather than soft blur spread. Where physical separation is required (such as modal sheets or the anchored mobile bottom bar), an ultra-diffused, ambient contact shadow is used: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(27, 38, 50, 0.04)`.
- **Selected & Interactive Depths:** Focused or engaged elements do not pop outward via deep shadows; they darken slightly in surface tone or gain an inner micro-inset hairline, reinforcing the physical metaphor of architectural linen and card stock.

## Shapes

Shapes reflect precision cutting with a rounded, welcoming profile that balances modern approachability with structured architecture.

- **Base Corner Radius (0.5rem / 8px):** Applied to form fields, data chips, segment indicators, and table item rows.
- **Card & Surface Radius (`rounded-lg` / 1rem / 16px):** Applied to summary modules, tenant profile wrappers, and monthly cash flow summaries.
- **Modal & Bottom Sheet Radius (`rounded-xl` / 1.5rem / 24px):** Applied to top edges of sliding drawers and presentation sheets on mobile.
- **Strict Prohibition:** Full pill shapes are reserved exclusively for circular indicator pips or avatar placeholders. Interactive buttons and tags maintain disciplined, rounded rectangular geometries.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid background `#1B2632`, text `#EEE9DF`. Padding: `0.75rem 1.25rem`. Rounded corners (`0.5rem`). For key actions (e.g., `Log Payment`, `Collect Rent`), the background may transition to `#FFB162` with text `#1B2632` and font weight `600`.
- **Secondary Action:** Transparent fill, `1px solid rgba(201, 193, 177, 0.7)`, text `#2C3B4D`. Hover or active state introduces a light wash of `#C9C1B1` at 20% opacity.
- **Destructive/Urgent:** Outline or subtle tint fill of `#A35139` at 10% opacity, text `#A35139`, with no loud neon red tones.

### Cards & Ledger Rows
- **Property Ledger Card:** Enclosed in `1px solid rgba(201, 193, 177, 0.4)`, background `#F7F4EE`. Features distinct zones: property header in Comfortaa, yield percentages and occupancy status in crisp Outfit uppercase chips.
- **Financial Rows:** Alternating data lines separated by `1px solid rgba(201, 193, 177, 0.3)`. Tabular figures aligned flush right with status indicators positioned left of the numeric total.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Badges:** Compact height (22px), `0.5rem` radius. Monospaced or uppercase Outfit (`10px`, weight `600`).
  - *Occupied / Paid:* Background `rgba(44, 59, 77, 0.08)`, text `#2C3B4D`.
  - *Pending / Action Needed:* Background `rgba(255, 177, 98, 0.15)`, text `#1B2632`.
  - *Overdue / Alert:* Background `rgba(163, 81, 57, 0.12)`, text `#A35139`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Height 44px for reliable mobile touch targets. Background `#F4EFE6`, border `1px solid #C9C1B1`. Active focus state transitions border to `#2C3B4D` without an outer glowing halo.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** `18px x 18px` squares with `4px` corner radius. Checked fill `#1B2632` with an `#EEE9DF` architectural checkmark.

### Mobile Bottom Navigation Bar
- Anchored at viewport base. Background `#EEE9DF` with a single `1px solid rgba(201, 193, 177, 0.5)` top rule. Four persistent items: Dashboard, Properties, Tenants, Payments.
- Active item uses `#1B2632` typography with a micro dot (`3px`) centered below the label. Inactive items use `#2C3B4D` at 45% opacity.
